Answers to the owner's direct questions:

- **Is the media studio reachable from the UI?** Only through Chat → `+` → the "Create media" section of the context sheet, and only for signed-in members. It is not a product in the sidebar and typing "اصنع لي صورة" in chat does nothing media-related. That is why it reads as "does not exist".
- **Does it poll?** Yes, with sane back-off, but forever (no deadline) and only while the studio sheet is open or the process is alive; after a relaunch nothing polls until the sheet is opened or a push is tapped.
- **Does it fetch the files with cookies?** Yes. `APIClient` uses `HTTPCookieStorage.shared` with `httpShouldSetCookies = true` (APIClient.swift:57–60, 199), so `firas_session` rides on `/api/image?key=`, `/api/video/file`, `/api/music/file`. Playback is from the downloaded local file, so AVPlayer never needs the cookie.
- **Can it save to Photos?** Yes for image and video (`saveToPhotos`, MediaStudioStore.swift:184–213; `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` is in Info.plist). Music has Share only.

## B. Findings

Severity: critical / major / minor. Category: compile-risk | crash | freeze-or-main-thread | contract-mismatch |
missing-feature-vs-web | background-cloud-first | ux | visual-design | rtl-arabic | ipad | accessibility | security.

### B.1 Swift 5 language-mode / concurrency (compile-risk)

1. **All files — minor — compile-risk.** The switch to Swift 5 + `minimal` + default nonisolated is safe for this group: both stores are explicitly `@MainActor` (`BrainStore.swift:4`, `MediaStudioStore.swift:5`), `MediaAssetRepository` (654) and `MediaLocalFileLoader` (MediaStudioScreen.swift:970) are actors, `FirasCompletionCue`/`NotificationCoordinator` are `@MainActor`, and every view inherits `@MainActor` from `View`. No file in this group needs a new `@MainActor`. Two things to watch: (a) the `nonisolated struct/enum/class` spellings (`BrainModels.swift:3+`, `BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:7,22,29`, `OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:213,456,523,581,597`, `MediaStudioModels.swift:3+`) need SE-0449, i.e. Swift 6.1+ — fine on Xcode 26, a hard error if the runner is ever pinned to Xcode 16.0–16.2; (b) `switch current { case ..<Duration.seconds(2): … }` (`MediaStudioStore.swift:590–597`) relies on range-pattern matching of `Duration` — valid, but replace with plain comparisons if a CI cycle ever fails there.
2. **`OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:241–246, 262` — minor — compile-risk.** `FileManager.unzipItem(at:to:skipCRC32:allowUncontainedSymlinks:)` and the throwing `Archive(url:accessMode:)` exist in ZIPFoundation 0.9.17+; `Package.resolved` pins 0.9.20 (`22787ff`). Keep the pin exact; do not "update to latest" on the runner.

### B.2 Brain

3. **`BrainStore.swift:33, 53–60, 104, 132–138` and `BrainScreen.swift:88–96` — major — contract-mismatch.** Guests are refused everywhere (`session.isAuthenticated` is `phase == .authenticated`, SessionStore.swift:37) with an ad-hoc string. The server and the web treat guests as first-class Brain users (3 docs, 120 pages/day, 120 answers/day; server-brain.md §2, §11, web-brain-ux.md §14). Fix: gate on `session.identityID != nil` (member or guest) and let the server's `403 signin_required` open the sign-up sheet; show `n/3` and `صفحات اليوم` for guests from `limits`.
4. **`BrainStore.swift:89` with `BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:92–104, 139` — major — contract-mismatch.** `ocr` reports **on-device Vision** pages. The server adds that number to the site-wide Gemini budget (`brainVisionCharge`, server.mjs:8468) — every native import of a scanned book silently burns `visionLeft` for all users although Gemini was never called (web-brain-ux.md §15.1, §18.1). Fix: send `ocr: 0` unless a page actually went through server vision; keep a local "read on device" badge if wanted.
5. **`BrainStore.swift:129–177` + `BrainScreen.swift:321–334` — critical — missing-feature-vs-web.** Brain never produces an answer. The store stops at retrieval and the screen lists chunks. The web pipeline (web-brain-ux.md §7, §9, §11) — whole-document read for members, harvest / compare / quiz / overview / reasoning routing, `/api/chat` `nomem` streaming with the grounding prompts, `[Sn]` chips, `firas-sources` fence, passage viewer with highlighted spans — is entirely absent. This is the "Brain is thin" complaint. Fix: implement a `BrainAsker` per web-brain-ux.md §15.2 and render answers in a thread; persist turns as `brainNb` chats.
6. **`BrainModels.swift:411–424` + `BrainScreen.swift:328–332` — major — ux / contract-mismatch.** `BrainHit` has no `near` field, and the search response appends up to 20 neighbour chunks (score × 0.30/0.22) after the genuine matches (server-brain.md §8.4). The UI renders all of them identically with a bookmark icon, so 8 real matches become ~28 "results", most of them not matches. Fix: add `let near: Bool?`, show neighbours only inside the passage reader (or collapsed under their parent).
7. **`BrainStore.swift:156–162` — minor — contract-mismatch.** `resultCount: 20` — the server clamps `k` to 1…12 (server.mjs:9110). Use 8 (12 for reasoning questions) like the web.
8. **`BrainScreen.swift:539–577` — major — accessibility.** `BrainHitCard` puts `.accessibilityLabel(BrainStrings.openPassage)` on the whole button, which *replaces* the title, page and text for VoiceOver: every result reads "Open original passage". Use `.accessibilityHint` for the action and let the content be the label (or `accessibilityElement(children: .combine)`).
9. **`BrainScreen.swift:192–213` + `BrainStrings.swift:27–28` + `Brain.xcstrings` (`brain.supported`, `brain.officeExport`) — minor — ux.** The "Native on-device reading" card lists only PDF / TXT / IMG and tells the user to export DOCX/PPTX/XLSX as PDF, while the importer accepts `.docx/.pptx/.xlsx` (BrainScreen.swift:395) and `OfficeDocumentExtractor` reads them. `BrainExtractionError.officeNeedsExport` (BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:26; BrainStore.swift:260–266) is dead. Replace the card with the web's `addHint` («PDF، Word، PowerPoint، Excel، نصوص، وصور»).
10. **`BrainScreen.swift:29–50` and `ShellSidebar.swift:238–245` — major — ux.** The sidebar lists `brainNb` conversations when Brain is selected, but `BrainScreen` does not read `ChatStore` at all, so selecting a Brain chat has no visible effect. Either hide the chat list for Brain until the thread exists, or (preferred, with finding 5) render the selected `brainNb` conversation as the Brain thread.
11. **`BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:90–114` — major — ux / freeze-or-main-thread (long-running, not main thread).** Scanned PDFs are OCR'd serially with `VNRecognizeTextRequest(.accurate)` at 1600×2200, one page at a time, with no cap, no stride, no progress counter (`importingFilename` only), and no cancellation (`importTask` is never cancelled from the UI). A 300-page scanned Arabic textbook means 5–15 minutes of "Reading and preparing source…" with the Add button disabled; backgrounding the app for more than ~30 s suspends the detached task and the later upload request times out (45 s), leaving a partial document server-side (server-brain.md §6.8). Fix: reproduce the web's policy (web-brain-ux.md §6.2): trigger OCR when non-whitespace < 40 **or** Arabic quality < 0.62 (current threshold is `< 12` chars, so most garbled-Arabic PDFs are never OCR'd), cap at 300 pages with even stride, run 2–3 pages concurrently, report `reading d/t → ocr d/t → uploading i/n`, offer Stop, and request `UIApplication.beginBackgroundTask` around the upload.
12. **`BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:164–175` — major — crash-adjacent / ux (verify on device).** `recognitionLanguages = ["ar","en-US"]` is set unconditionally. If the installed Vision revision does not list `ar` in `supportedRecognitionLanguages()`, `perform` throws; on the PDF path the error is swallowed (`try?` at 99) and the page is dropped, so a scanned Arabic PDF becomes `emptyDocument`; on the image path the raw `VNError` string is shown. Nobody can run this locally, so guard it: query `VNRecognizeTextRequest.supportedRecognitionLanguages()` at runtime, fall back to `automaticallyDetectsLanguage = true` (iOS 16+) and to server vision (`/api/chat` with the OCR prompt, server-brain.md §6.9) when Vision returns < 20 chars for a page that has ink.
13. **`BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:143–162` — minor — contract-mismatch.** Plain text is split into 12 000-char blocks with `unit: .section`; the web uses fixed 3 000-char blocks with `unit: page` (web-brain-ux.md §6.2, §17). The same file cites different "pages" on iPhone and web. Match 3 000 / `page`.
14. **`BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:177–198` and `OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:434–453` — minor — freeze-or-main-thread (CPU, off-main).** Both `chunkText` loops call `remaining.count` (O(n) grapheme walk over a `Substring`) on every iteration, giving O(n²/limit) — an 8 M-char DOCX is ~2.7 G character steps, i.e. tens of seconds on a phone, and the text path has **no size cap** at all (a 50 MB log file is read whole into a `String`). Track `utf16` offsets or slice with a single forward pass; cap text input (web has no cap either, but the server refuses > 8 M chars/doc anyway — refuse locally at that size).
15. **`BrainStore.swift:76–77, 216–217` — minor — freeze-or-main-thread.** After extraction, `uploadParts` runs on the main actor and calls `.text.count` on every page (up to 8 M characters) — a visible hitch of a few hundred ms on a large document. Move the split into the detached extraction task or pre-compute counts there.
16. **`BrainStore.swift:203–241` — minor — contract-mismatch.** Parts are 680 000 chars / 950 records and an oversized single page group **throws** `invalidRequest("One citable page…")`; the web uses 700 000 / 1 000 and lets a big group become its own part (web-brain-ux.md §17). Only an XLSX sheet record can approach this today (450 000 chars), but align anyway.
17. **`OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:239–246` — major — ux / performance.** The whole archive is written to disk and **fully expanded** (media, fonts, embedded videos — up to 160 MB) before any XML is parsed. A 60 MB lecture PPTX takes long and doubles its disk footprint per import. Iterate `archive` and `extract(entry, consumer:)` only `word/document.xml`, `ppt/presentation.xml` + rels + `ppt/slides/*.xml` (+ notes), `xl/workbook.xml` + rels + `xl/sharedStrings.xml` + `xl/worksheets/*.xml`.
18. **`OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:363–390` — major — contract-mismatch (citation honesty).** Sheets are ordered by the digits in `sheetN.xml` and labelled with `workbook.xml`'s `<sheet name>` list **by position**. After a user reorders or deletes sheets in Excel, `sheet3.xml` can be the first tab, so citations carry the wrong sheet name. Resolve `r:id` through `xl/_rels/workbook.xml.rels` like the web (web-brain-ux.md §6.2 XLSX). Same for PPTX slide order (`ppt/presentation.xml` `sldIdLst` via rels) at 315–323.
19. **`OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:287–309, 311–345, 347–400` — minor — contract-mismatch.** DOCX: no heading → label, 12 000-char cut instead of 4 000 sentence-aware, tables/lists flattened to bare lines. PPTX: slide-number / date / footer placeholders are indexed as text, no speaker notes, label is the first paragraph rather than the title placeholder, table cells become separate paragraphs. XLSX: dates stay as serial numbers, cells joined with `\t` instead of ` | `, no repeated header per 700-char group. Each of these changes what the citation says compared with the same file on the web.
20. **`BrainDocumentExtractor.swift:144–146` — minor — rtl-arabic.** Text decoding falls back to UTF-16 then Windows-1252. Arabic `.txt` exported from old Windows tools is Windows-1256; it will decode as mojibake instead of failing. Try `.windowsArabic` (`CFStringEncodings.windowsArabic`) before 1252, or refuse when the result has no Arabic/Latin letters.
21. **`BrainScreen.swift:289–303` — minor — ux.** The ask field is a vertical-axis `TextField` with `.onSubmit`; Return inserts a newline on that configuration, so "submit" only works through the arrow button. Match the web (Ctrl/Cmd+Enter sends, Return newlines) or set `.submitLabel(.send)` on a single-line field.
22. **`BrainScreen.swift:30–49, 358–376` — minor — rtl-arabic.** `layoutDirection` is applied to `content` (line 34) and the banner (47) but not to the `NavigationStack`/toolbar, so in Arabic the sidebar button stays on the left and the profile button on the right (the shell forces `.leftToRight` at the root, FirasAppShell.swift:47). Consistent with the other products, but note it when the shell decision is revisited.
23. **`BrainScreen.swift:354–356` — minor — rtl-arabic / i18n.** `deleteAlertTitle` is a hard-coded ar/en switch outside the catalog; the web has no confirm at all (native confirm is fine, but put the string in `Brain.xcstrings`).
24. **`BrainStrings.swift` / `Brain.xcstrings` — minor — ux.** All 27 keys exist in both languages (verified), but none is the web's verbatim copy (web-brain-ux.md §5.1, §7.9). Port `brainT()`.
25. **`BrainModels.swift:305–316, 440–451` — minor — crash-adjacent.** `kind`/`unit` decode as strict enums; one legacy record with an unknown value fails the whole `/api/brain/docs` decode and the library shows an error banner. Decode leniently with `text`/`page` fallbacks (server-brain.md §4 note).
26. **Brain — major — background-cloud-first / missing-feature-vs-web.** Nothing in Brain is durable: imports are foreground-only (finding 11) and there is no `brainask` job, so the notification copy for Brain in `NotificationCoordinator.swift:393–397` can never fire. For long asks (harvest sweeps, whole reads) use `POST /api/chat/job kind:"brainask"` for members (server-brain.md §13) or at least hold a background task around the answer stream.
27. **Brain — minor — missing-feature-vs-web.** No mic on the ask box (the web's Brain composer has the shared dictation recorder, web-brain-ux.md §5.2), no tier choice for the (future) answer, no page-range chip, no pins/excluded-set (native uses an *include* set that is not persisted: `selectedDocumentIDs`, BrainStore.swift:10).

### B.3 Media

28. **`MediaStudioStore.swift` (whole) + `MediaStudioScreen.swift:23–24` — critical — missing-feature-vs-web.** Creations never become conversation turns. They live only in `UserDefaults` on this device (24 entries, `historyLimit` at line 22), no `firas-image|video|music` fence is ever written, and the iOS chat renders none of those fences from `/api/chats` (a picture made on the web shows as raw fenced JSON in the iOS chat). Nothing reaches the sidebar, other devices, sharing or the web gallery (web-media-ux.md §12.2, §14.1–2). Fix: a studio creation must write a user turn + assistant fence into a chosen/new conversation and the chat renderer must dispatch the three fences (web-media-ux.md §9 order).
29. **`AddContextSheet.swift:350–395` → `ChatScreen.swift:246–253` — major — ux.** The only entry to media creation is a row inside the attachment sheet, under "Photos" and "Files". Users looking for "make an image" look in the sidebar or type it in chat. Add a sidebar product/tab (or a composer chip) and the in-chat classifier (web-media-ux.md §1, §12.1) so «اصنع لي صورة…» works.
30. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:391–446` — major — ux / background-cloud-first.** No polling deadline. The server answers `running` forever for an id it has forgotten (restart, or the 1-hour sweep of a failed job; server-media.md §0.6, §6.2). A lost job spins for the life of the process and — because `remove` refuses active creations (173–174) — can never be dismissed. Add the web deadlines (image/video 20 min, music 10 min) → `failed` with code `timeout` (the string already exists at 634/642) while keeping `jobID`, and allow Remove on timed-out items.
31. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:95–113` and `MediaStudioScreen.swift:79–84` — major — background-cloud-first.** `resumeIfNeeded()` is called only by the studio sheet. After a relaunch, an in-flight job is neither polled nor downloaded until the user opens `+` → Create media (or taps the push). The Agent/Code stores are resumed from the shell on notification (FirasAppShell.swift:242–244); media is not even there. Call `mediaStudioStore.resumeIfNeeded()` from `FirasAppShell` `.task(id: session.identityID)` and on `scenePhase == .active`, and on the `.ai` notification branch.
32. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:478–515` with `APIClient.swift:53–63, 108–135` — major — crash / freeze-or-main-thread (memory).** Completed files are fetched with `session.data(for:)` — the whole body in memory — under `timeoutIntervalForResource = 180`. A Wan-3 clip can be up to 200 MB (server-media.md §2.1); on cellular that exceeds 180 s → three failures → `result_download_pending`, and when it does succeed the 200 MB `Data` plus the atomic write spike memory on older devices. Use a `URLSessionDownloadTask` to a file (or stream video/music through `AVURLAsset` with `AVURLAssetHTTPCookiesKey`, Range works — server-media.md §2.3) and keep the in-memory path for images only.
33. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:323–367` — minor — ux.** The job-start retry loop never terminates: after `attempt >= 3` it only sets `errorMessage` and keeps retrying every 15 s while the server keeps 5xx-ing (`retryable` at 610–624). Cap at ~6 attempts → `failed`.
34. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:220–257, 370–388` + `MediaStudioScreen.swift:118–125, 185–210` — major — missing-feature-vs-web / contract-mismatch.** No prompt pipeline. The raw typed text is sent: for images no English rewrite / `pickImageShape`; for video no rewrite and no first frame; for music the Arabic *description* typed into "Describe the mood, voice, and rhythm…" is sent as ACE-Step **style tags** and, with lyrics empty, the song is instrumental — while the placeholder promises «اكتب الكلمات، أو اتركها لفِراس» ("or leave them to Firas") and nothing writes lyrics. The web derives English tags (`musicStyleFor`) and model-written lyrics with the `STYLE:` line (web-media-ux.md §6.1–6.3). Port the three pipelines; until then relabel the music field honestly.
35. **`MediaStudioModels.swift:756–778` — minor — contract-mismatch / visual-design.** `portrait` 1024×1280 renders as **4:5** and `banner` 1280×640 as **16:9** on the Replicate rung (server-media.md §1.6). Use 960×1280 (3:4) and 1280×549 (21:9), or relabel.
36. **`MediaStudioModels.swift:787–790` — minor — missing-feature-vs-web.** `MediaVideoJobRequest` has no `image` (first frame); the web's headline video feature ("an attached photo becomes the first frame") is absent. Add `image: String?` carrying `data:image/jpeg;base64,…` ≤ ~8 MB (re-encode HEIC).
37. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:626–644` with `APIClient.swift:43–45, 168–172` — minor — ux / contract-mismatch.** `ServerErrorEnvelope` keeps only `error`, so `limit`, `used`, `windowMin`, `freesInMin`, `feature` never reach the store; `rate_window` shows «الإنشاء مزدحم الآن» with no minutes and `daily_limit` cannot print 8. Extend the envelope and use the web sentences (web-media-ux.md §11).
38. **`MediaStudioModels.swift:827` + `MediaStudioStore.swift:557–560, 571–583` — minor — ux.** `localFileURL` is persisted as an absolute path. The app container UUID changes on every App Store / TestFlight update, so after an update `hasReadableLocalAsset` is false for every completed creation → all are re-downloaded (`/api/image` is rate-limited 240/min) and `remove` can no longer delete the old files. Persist the filename only and rebuild the URL from `MediaAssetRepository.directory`.
39. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:562–569` — minor — ux.** `trimHistory` drops creations past 24 without deleting their files → orphaned multi-megabyte files in Application Support. Delete on trim; also exclude the directory from iCloud backup.
40. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:841–904` — major — ux.** Music playback uses a bare `AVPlayer` with no `AVAudioSession` category: with the ring switch on silent the song is muted (default `.soloAmbient`), playback stops on lock (no `audio` background mode), there is no scrubber/elapsed time, and two cards can play at once. Set `.playback` before play, one player at a time, add a slider + `0:00 / 3:12` in LTR digits (web-media-ux.md §6.4, §12.1.5).
41. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:645–659` — minor — ux.** A failed card's headline is `MediaStrings.retry` ("Try again") with a warning icon, followed by a "Try again" button; `creation.errorCode` is never shown, so the user never learns *why* (limit, engine, timeout). Show the mapped sentence.
42. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:785–820` — minor — freeze-or-main-thread.** `UIImage(data:)` is created in the task but decoded lazily on the main thread at first draw; a 2K PNG (3–5 MB) hitches the scroll. Use `await UIImage(data:)?.byPreparingForDisplay()` (or `preparingThumbnail(of:)` for the card) inside the task.
43. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:563–590` — major — missing-feature-vs-web / ux.** No full-screen viewer, no caption, no "regenerate" for a completed picture (identical inputs are a free cache hit, so a real re-roll needs a changed prompt), no gallery, no ZIP/"download all", no video download name. The result feed is a list of prompt-text cards.
44. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:43–59, 404–420, 426, 463, 572, 948` — major — visual-design.** Every content card is a `GlassSurface` (`.glassEffect(.regular…)` on iOS 26, `.ultraThinMaterial` + border on 18–25) stacked inside a `GlassEffectContainer` over a flat gradient. Apple's Liquid Glass guidance reserves glass for the floating controls/navigation layer, not for scrolling content; `.regular` glass over a plain background reads as opaque grey slabs — exactly the "glass is not transparent enough" complaint. Keep glass for the kind picker, the create button and the toolbar (use `.clear` or `.regular.interactive()`), and put results and the prompt form on `palette.surface` with a hairline, so the actual pictures/videos are the design. Same pattern in `BrainScreen` (193, 216, 272, 406, 438, 546, 655).
45. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:122, 208` + `MediaStudioStore.swift:380, 386` — minor — contract-mismatch.** Video presets 5/10/15/30 (web sends `quota.seconds` = 10); music presets 30/60/90/180 default 90 (web always 150; the engine wait is 180 s so 180-second songs risk `engine_failed`, server-media.md §3.1). Read `seconds` from `GET /api/video/quota`; default music to 150, cap the picker at 150.
46. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:303–330` — minor — rtl-arabic.** `.environment(\.layoutDirection…)` (line 72) is applied to the `ZStack`, so the toolbar (X on `topBarLeading`, account on trailing) does not mirror in Arabic; the principal title stack does. Same root cause as finding 22.
47. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:371–383` — minor — rtl-arabic.** Duration chips print Western digits with a trailing «ث»/«د»; the web uses Arabic-Indic digits for counts in Arabic (`arDigits`). Format with `Locale(identifier: "ar")` number formatting.
48. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:519–561` — minor — accessibility.** `AspectPresetButton` is `frame(width: 74, height: 70)` with `.lineLimit(1)`; at accessibility Dynamic Type sizes labels truncate. Let the chips grow (`ViewThatFits` or `minWidth`).
49. **`MediaStudioScreen.swift:785–820` — minor — accessibility.** The result image's label is "Result"; announce the prompt (≤120 chars) as the web does for `alt`.
50. **`ChatScreen.swift:140–149` (presentation of this group's screen) — minor — ipad.** On iPad the `.sheet` with `.large` detent becomes a ~540 pt form sheet; a "studio" with a 16:9 video and a results feed is cramped and the hero card wastes a third of it. Present full-screen on regular width, or make Media a real product with `NavigationSplitView` (library | create).
51. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:184–213` — minor — ux.** Music cannot be saved (only shared); the web offers Download for all three kinds. Offer "Save to Files" via `fileExporter` for music (and video).
52. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:453–459` + `FirasCompletionCue.swift:157–174` — minor — ux.** The completion cue holds the reveal for 3 s after two soft impacts. That is the requested "haptic before completion", but a 3-second artificial wait on every finished picture feels like a stall; Claude's cue is ~0.5 s before the final message. Consider 600–900 ms. (Cue lives in the DesignSystem group; the delay is felt here.)
53. **`MediaStudioStore.swift:47–90` — minor — background-cloud-first.** Push routing works only because the APNs payload carries `mediaKind`; once creations live in conversations (finding 28) the pointer table must also yield the `chatId`, because media pushes carry none (web-media-ux.md §13.15).
54. **Security — no findings that block.** Temp workspace is created per import and removed (`defer`, OfficeDocumentExtractor.swift:237); XML external entities are disabled (419); zip traversal/symlink/size guards are correct (261–285); `MediaAssetRepository.remove` refuses paths outside its directory (687–694); identifiers are sanitised (680, FirasAPI.swift:483–492); cookies stay in the system jar. One note: prompts and lyrics are persisted in `UserDefaults` in clear text (MediaStudioStore.swift:576–583) — acceptable, but Application Support + file protection would be tidier once history moves to conversations.
