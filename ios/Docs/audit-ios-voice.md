## B. Root causes of "entering the call kicks me out / the app freezes"

Ranked by likelihood, each with the evidence in the code. The crashes are uncaught Objective-C exceptions raised by AVFoundation — Swift `try` does not catch them, so the process terminates ("kicked out").

1. **`AVAudioPlayerNode.play()` while the engine is not running → `NSInternalInconsistencyException` (`required condition is false: _engine->IsRunning()`).** `schedulePlayback` (`LiveVoiceController.swift:465-468`) calls `player.scheduleBuffer(buffer)` then `player.play()` for every incoming audio packet with no check of `audioEngine.isRunning`. The engine stops on its own in three ordinary situations the code never observes: (a) an **audio route change** — AirPods connecting, headphones plugged, and on some devices the `overrideOutputAudioPort(.speaker)` issued *after* `engine.start()` at line 436 — posts `AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange` and stops the engine; (b) an **AVAudioSession interruption** (incoming phone call, Siri, alarm) stops it before `VoiceCallView` reacts (`VoiceCallView.swift:66-71` only calls `end()` afterwards); (c) **hang-up races** — `stopAudioAndTransport` (`:502-520`) stops the engine and only then `await transport?.close()`, while `.audio` events already hopped to the main actor by `receiveLoop` are still queued and land after the engine is stopped. In all three the next packet crashes the app. This is the strongest candidate for "kicks me out" both at entry (route settle right after start) and mid-call.
2. **No echo cancellation → the model hears itself → endless `interrupted` / `player.stop()` loop.** `configureAudioSession` sets mode `.voiceChat` (`:524-528`) but never calls `audioEngine.inputNode.setVoiceProcessingEnabled(true)`; AVAudioEngine only gets Apple's AEC/AGC through the voice-processing IO unit — the session mode by itself does not add it. With `.defaultToSpeaker` the phone speaker feeds straight back into the mic; Gemini's server VAD sends `serverContent.interrupted`, `handle(.interrupted)` (`:385-392`) stops playback, the model restarts, and every reply is cut after a second — a call that sounds broken and "stuck". The web hand-rolls an echo floor for exactly this reason (`Docs/server-voice.md` §7.4); iOS has neither AEC nor the guard.
3. **"Connecting…" forever with no way out but End (perceived freeze).** `GeminiLiveTransport.connect` (`:129-149`) builds a `URLSession` with `waitsForConnectivity = true` and the default resource timeout (7 days), sends the setup, and returns; nothing waits for `setupComplete` with a deadline (the web waits 10 s, `Docs/server-voice.md` §7.5 item 8). On a flaky network, a refused setup, or a spent token (`uses: 1` — every retry needs a fresh mint) the screen stays on "Connecting" with Mute/Speaker disabled (`VoiceCallView.swift:119, 136`). A socket that closes does report `voice_connection_lost` (`:231-235`); a socket that opens and never answers does not.
4. **Backgrounding ends the call by design, and the app cannot run audio in the background anyway.** `VoiceCallView.swift:63-65` calls `controller.handleInterruption()` → `end()` on `didEnterBackgroundNotification`; `Info.plist` has no `UIBackgroundModes` → `audio`. Locking the phone, opening a notification, or switching apps kills the call; if the hook were simply removed, iOS would suspend the app within seconds and the socket + engine would die silently. This is the direct cause of "the call kicks me out when I leave" and of the "things must keep working after I leave the app" complaint.
5. **Out-of-order microphone frames.** The tap (`:426-432`) spawns one unstructured `Task { @concurrent in await transport.sendPCM16(...) }` per 100 ms buffer. Unstructured tasks carry no ordering guarantee and `sendPCM16` suspends on `socket.send`, so with actor reentrancy frame N+1 can be sent before frame N. The model receives scrambled audio → poor recognition and spurious VAD triggers. The web feeds one ordered loop.
6. **Main-thread stalls at entry.** `configureAudioSession()` (`setCategory` + `setActive(true)`, typically 50–400 ms) and `audioEngine.start()` (100–300 ms) run synchronously on the main actor inside `start()` (`:332, :435`) during the `fullScreenCover` presentation animation, and the session is activated *before* the token mint (up to 30 s with `waitsForConnectivity`). Not a hard freeze, but a visible hitch, and the user's music is cut for nothing if the mint fails.
7. **GPU-bound orb (perceived freeze on older phones).** `VoiceReactiveOrb` runs a 30 fps `TimelineView` (`VoiceCallView.swift:216`) that re-renders a 238 pt circle with `.blur(radius: 31)` (`:228-232`), three scaled rings, an angular gradient, and — on iOS 26 — a `GlassSurface` (`.glassEffect`) whose geometry is scaled every frame (`:260-269`). Liquid Glass samples its backdrop each frame; continuously animating its geometry is what Apple tells you not to do. On iPhone 11–13 this drops frames and makes End feel unresponsive.
8. **Swift 5 language mode / minimal concurrency (compile-risk).** `LiveVoiceController` and `PreferencesStore` are explicitly `@MainActor`; `VoiceCallView`, `VoiceReactiveOrb` and the controls are `View`s (implicitly `@MainActor` through the `@MainActor protocol View` in the iOS 18+ SDK, so the `@State` initial value `LiveVoiceController()` is main-actor code); the transport and token client are actors; the tap closure's task only touches `self` through `await`. Nothing in this group **needs** a new `@MainActor` after the mode switch. Two constructs to verify on the first CI run, both cheap to pre-empt: `@concurrent` on the closure at `:428` (SE-0461, Swift 6.2 — replace with `Task.detached` or, better, the ordered stream from C5), and `nonisolated enum/struct` at file scope (`:6, :15, :20, :29`, accepted since Swift 6.1 / SE-0449, fine on Xcode 26). `.allowBluetoothHFP` (`:527`) is the iOS 26 SDK spelling of `.allowBluetooth` and compiles on Xcode 26 for an iOS 18 target.

Suspected and ruled out: `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` (present); the tap format (`inputNode.outputFormat(forBus: 0)` is queried after the session is configured, which is the correct pairing); the converter (there is no `AVAudioConverter`; the hand-rolled linear interpolation is numerically safe — samples are clamped before the `Int16` conversion, `upper` is clamped, `sourceCount == 1` is handled); `access_token` on the Constrained endpoint (matches the web and the server's `auth_tokens` mint); force-unwraps (`AVAudioFormat(...)!` at `:417-422` cannot fail for those arguments); actor deadlock (`receiveLoop` suspends on `socket.receive()`, so the transport actor stays free).

## D. The corrected design (exact)

### D1. Ladder and mint
1. `POST /api/live/token { voice }` via `APIClient`. Decode `{ provider, token, model, voice?, maxMs, guest, startWithinMs }`.
2. `provider == "openai"` → `OpenAIRealtimeTransport`; no `session.created` within 12 s → teardown → mint again `{ prefer: "gemini", voice }` (free inside 90 s) → `GeminiLiveTransport`; failure → three-hop. Every exit records `LiveDiagnostics { engine, model, reason, detail, at }`; signed-in users get a small toast naming the engine when it is not OpenAI.
3. Quota / rate handling per C7; Gemini 1008/1011 cooldown per C9.

### D2. OpenAI Realtime over WebSocket (primary)
- URL `wss://api.openai.com/v1/realtime?model=<token.model>`, header `Authorization: Bearer <ek_…>`, **no** `OpenAI-Beta` header (GA vocabulary). `URLSessionWebSocketTask`; `sendPing` every 20 s.
- Wait for `session.created`; assert `session.instructions` starts with "You are Firas" and `audio.input.turn_detection.type == "semantic_vad"`; otherwise tear down and fall back (the persona is server-owned; never `session.update` instructions / voice / turn_detection).
- Send once: `session.update { session: { type: "realtime", audio: { input: { format: { type: "audio/pcm", rate: 24000 } }, output: { format: { type: "audio/pcm", rate: 24000 } } } } }`, then the greeting `response.create { response: { instructions: "Greet the caller warmly in ONE short sentence in Arabic|English, then stop and listen." } }`.
- Up: `input_audio_buffer.append { audio: base64 }` every 100 ms — 2 400 Int16 LE mono samples (4 800 bytes) at 24 kHz; never `commit`, never `response.create` for normal turns.
- Down: any `type` containing `"audio.delta"` → base64 → Int16 24 kHz → playback queue; `response.output_audio_transcript.*` and `conversation.item.input_audio_transcription.completed` → captions (optionally persisted into the chat).
- Phases: `speech_started` → listening (+ barge-in flush if enabled), `speech_stopped` → thinking, `response.created` → thinking + mic gate closed, first delta → speaking, `response.done` (any status) → listening + reopen the gate after 280 ms; 20 s safety reopen; mute always wins.
- Barge-in (setting, default off like the web): on `speech_started` while audio is queued → stop the player, drop the queue, `conversation.item.truncate { item_id, content_index: 0, audio_end_ms: playedMs }` with `playedMs = renderedFrames / 24`.
- Two clocks: hard `max(60 s, maxMs) − 1.5 s` (the client is the only ceiling on OpenAI); idle 45 s from `lastVoiceAt` (bumped on the four voice events and every output delta).

### D3. Gemini Live (fallback)
Keep the existing transport; fix per C3, C5, C8, C9; add the echo guard (`liveBargeDecision`: RMS 0.055, ×3 over the echo floor, 3 consecutive frames, 350 ms hangover, silence substitution) even with VPIO on; `interrupted` → flush playback and zero the echo state.

### D4. AVAudioEngine graph
```
AVAudioSession (off-main): .playAndRecord, .voiceChat, [.allowBluetoothHFP] (+ .defaultToSpeaker only when "speaker" is chosen),
    preferredSampleRate 24_000, preferredIOBufferDuration 0.02, setActive(true)
engine.inputNode.setVoiceProcessingEnabled(true)          // AEC/AGC — FIRST
inputFormat = inputNode.outputFormat(forBus: 0)             // read AFTER VPIO
upConverter = AVAudioConverter(from: inputFormat, to: Int16 mono @ 24_000 (OpenAI) | 16_000 (Gemini))
inputNode.installTap(bufferSize: inputFormat.sampleRate * 0.1, format: inputFormat) → convert → AsyncStream<Data> (bufferingNewest 8)
player: AVAudioPlayerNode → mainMixerNode with standardFormat(24_000, 1 ch, Float32); Int16 deltas → Float32 buffers;
    scheduleBuffer in order (forward-only queue); track renderedFrames via lastRenderTime for truncate;
    last-buffer completion + 160 ms debounce → "listening"
engine.prepare(); engine.start()
Observers: AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange (rebuild connection + tap, restart), routeChange (re-query formats; hide the speaker toggle on BT/headphones),
    interruption (.began pause/gate, .ended resume)
```
Mic gate / mute: replace suppressed frames with zeros of equal length (continuous stream for the server VAD). Orb level: RMS of the last scheduled/captured buffer, published ≤ 20 Hz, `sqrt` before display.

### D5. Teardown checklist (idempotent, `isEnding` first)
1. `isEnding = true`; cancel hard, idle, safety, drain tasks.
2. Stop the player, drop the queue, zero the echo state.
3. `inputNode.removeTap(onBus: 0)`; finish the `AsyncStream`.
4. `engine.stop()`.
5. Close the socket with `.normalClosure`; invalidate its `URLSession`.
6. `setActive(false, options: .notifyOthersOnDeactivation)` off-main.
7. Null every handle; publish `.ended`; if backgrounded post the local notification.
8. A failed attempt runs 1–7 before the next rung.

### D6. Background and notifications
`UIBackgroundModes: audio`; no `didEnterBackground → end`; sockets and engine stay alive; on cap / idle / drop in the background → `UNUserNotificationCenter` "انتهت المكالمة" with the reason; on foreground return re-sync the UI from controller state. Haptics: light impact on `.connected`; the `FirasCompletionCue` double pulse before the "Call ended" reveal (foreground only, respects Reduce Motion). CallKit is not required.
